\section{What Compression Does to Capabilities}
\label{sec:observed}

The measurements fix what a law has to reproduce.

\paragraph{Damage is uneven across capabilities, and the ordering is not fixed.} Across twelve public
models the same pruning density leaves QA the least-damaged capability in some series and the
most-damaged in others (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_hetero}); on the controlled Pythia panel, math and code
respond to density along one shared shape with a capability-specific scale, while the QA response
changes sign across density, so no scale of a shared curve represents it
(Figs.~\ref{fig:heterogeneity} and \ref{fig:pythia_responses}).

\paragraph{Size and stage of the source shape the response, inside a range.} Parameter count and
pretraining tokens both enter the pruning amplitude for math and code and neither rescues QA. Inside
the development range the source-conditioned
forms, a per-density regression and the median density curve agree within 0.05 nats at unseen
stages of a 1B source; at a 6.9B source, five times the development range, the source-conditioned
forms lose to the median curve on math (0.30 against 0.12 nats) and QA (0.91 against 0.12) and tie
on code (\S\ref{sec:general}).



\paragraph{Distillation damage is a property of the protocol as much as of the method.} With everything but the
learning rate held fixed, the loss increase on math and code is monotone in it: at $5\times10^{-5}$ the 1B student is essentially unharmed ($+0.003$ and $+0.007$
nats) while still gaining $0.79$ nats on QA, and at $1\times10^{-4}$ the increase is an order of
magnitude larger ($+0.051$ and $+0.052$); at $1\times10^{-4}$ and $2\times10^{-4}$ the 4B student
degrades about twice as much as the 1B on math, and at the lowest rate the 1B is nearly unharmed
while the 4B is not ($+0.058$). Every distillation number that follows names its rate.



\paragraph{Reusing a small pool is expensive, the price rises with student size, and QA pays most of
it.} At one rate and a matched budget near 200k supervised tokens (Fig.~\ref{fig:responses_v2}), a
pool reused about twelve times costs QA $+0.29$, $+2.55$ and $+4.85$ nats on students of 100M, 700M
and 3.2B non-embedding parameters, while nine times more independent data turns the same budget into
a gain near one nat on each; math and code damage falls monotonically as well. Two pool seeds per
cell put the pool-draw noise near 0.02 nats on code and 0.13 on QA.

\paragraph{The cost transfers across QA distributions; the gain does not.} Scored on a fresh 2Wiki
sample, MuSiQue and TriviaQA, all three distributions worsen at high reuse and improve monotonically as
reuse falls, for every student, so the reuse cost is not an artefact of the training distribution.
Only 2Wiki crosses into a gain; MuSiQue flattens near zero and TriviaQA stays positive at every reuse
level measured, and the 4B student is damaged about twice as much as the 1B at matched reuse on all
three.

\paragraph{What a law must reproduce.} A relation for these responses has to carry a
capability-specific shape under pruning, a threshold with a configuration-dependent position under
quantization, and, under distillation, a reuse cost that is monotone in $E=T/D_U$, grows with student
size, and separates by evaluation distribution in sign. The relations of \S\ref{sec:laws} are judged against these features and \S\ref{sec:why}
asks what accounts for them.
