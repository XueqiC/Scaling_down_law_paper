\section{From Laws to Capability-Specific Selection}
\label{sec:selection}


Given a reference model, a capability, and a budget, which method
and setting preserves that capability best? The decision is
\begin{equation}
(m^{*},\boldsymbol\theta_m^{*})=\arg\min_{m,\;\boldsymbol\theta_m\in\mathcal F_m(B)}\;
\widehat L_{m,c}(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\theta_m),
\label{eq:selection}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal F_m(B)$ holds the settings of method $m$ within
budget $B$. 



\paragraph{Retrospective validation.} On the controlled panel (17 states, budgets from 0.2 to 1,
every law refit with the target held out) the law-based choice is within 0.19, 0.17 and 0.42 nats of
the oracle for math, code and QA and picks the oracle's method in 96\%, 95\% and 73\% of 289 cells;
quantization-only is close behind (0.21, 0.19, 0.49).

\paragraph{Prospective confirmation of a frozen rule.} A complete selection rule was locked
(Appendix Table~\ref{tab:locked_rule}; source-free predictors on new states), every candidate's
predicted loss and the four maps were frozen for four fresh Pythia states (160M, 410M and 1.4B at
step 32k, 1B at 64k; 18 configurations each, weights hashed against every earlier revision), and
only then measured (Fig.~\ref{fig:rule_main}). Over 68 cells per objective the frozen-rule maps are
within 0.00001, 0.004 and 0.14 nats of the oracle for math, code and QA, below the source-conditioned
maps (0.015, 0.11, 0.21) and quantization-only under the same candidates (0.007, 0.010, 0.25), meeting
the registered criterion; the rule beats quantization-only on three states and trails it on 160M@32k
(0.22 against 0.13). The math and code margins come almost entirely from the one state with
historical distillation candidates; on the three newly measured states the mean regrets differ by
less than $10^{-5}$ nats (Table~\ref{tab:rule_decomp}).

The planned independent validation of the selection rule across model families, with fresh
distillation candidates alongside pruning and quantization candidates under one feasible budget, was
not completed in this study; the prospective result above therefore establishes the rule on four
Pythia states under nominal storage budgets, and no single historical distillation candidate carries
a claim beyond that panel.
