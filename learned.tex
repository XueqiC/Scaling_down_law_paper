\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:learned}

This study developed predictive scaling-down laws for capability-conditioned loss under pruning,
quantization and distillation under one validation standard. Three findings organise the results:
capability responses differ in shape, so a law may share a curve across math and code but not with
question answering; predicting new configurations of measured models and transferring to new model
states are different tasks, served by initial-state inputs and by a source-free configuration curve
respectively; and the data requirement of distillation is defined by training exposure, student and
evaluation distribution together. On these findings rest method-specific relations with their
tested ranges (Table~\ref{tab:main-prediction-v2}), a rectangle design that measured the
budget-by-reuse interaction directly, a second-order account of compression damage, and a selection
rule confirmed prospectively on four fresh states, all with the status they had before their
outcomes were measured.
