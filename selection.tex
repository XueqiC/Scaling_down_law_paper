\section{From Laws to Capability-Specific Selection}
\label{sec:selection}


The relations turn configuration choice into a computation: given a reference model, a capability
and a budget, the decision is
\begin{equation}
(m^{*},\boldsymbol\theta_m^{*})=\arg\min_{m,\;\boldsymbol\theta_m\in\mathcal F_m(B)}\;
\widehat L_{m,c}(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\theta_m),
\label{eq:selection}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal F_m(B)$ holds the settings of method $m$ within budget $B$. On the controlled panel the law-based choice selects the method of the measured best candidate in
96\%, 95\% and 73\% of 289 cells for math, code and QA. A rule was then locked and its maps frozen
for four fresh Pythia states, where they come within 0.00001, 0.004 and 0.14 nats of the best
candidate, below quantization-only under the same candidates
(Appendix Fig.~\ref{fig:rule_main}). Those distillation candidates were historical, so a second
confirmation repeated the test across two families with candidates trained in the same round.

Four references outside every earlier fit, two Pythia checkpoints at a new training stage and two
Gemma-3 models, each received twelve candidates at seventeen storage budgets: four pruning
densities, six quantization settings, the dense model, and one new student distilled under the
recipe on which the delivered relation was fitted. The learned weights of every reference and student base were checked against all thirty earlier
revisions, and the predicted loss of every candidate and both maps were sealed before any student
was trained or any candidate compressed. Write the opportunity at a budget as the loss of the best feasible quantization candidate minus
that of the best feasible candidate of any method, and the regret of a policy as the loss of its
own frozen choice minus that same best candidate. Question answering
carries an opportunity of 0.12 to 0.89 nats, and the best candidate there is the new student in 59
of 68 cells; the rule selects it in 63 and gives up 0.000 nats on three references and 0.027 on the
fourth, against 0.41 to 1.84 nats for quantization-only under the same candidates. For mathematics,
code and the largest capability increase the opportunity is at most 0.047 nats, so quantization
already supplies a near-best candidate, the rule ties it, and in three of 256 cells trails it by at
most 0.019 nats. All four references meet the registered criterion. Cross-method selection is
therefore worth its measurements for a capability that responds to added data, and neutral for the
capabilities that do not respond to it.
