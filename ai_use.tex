\section*{AI Use Statement}
We used generative AI tools to polish the writing and to help implement experiment code, which
we verified by unit tests and by regenerating every reported number from the saved result files;
the research questions, the experimental design, the analysis and the scientific writing are the
authors' own. The distillation experiments are a separate matter: their training
data are teacher traces generated by a commercial language model through its interface, the object of study rather than an authoring aid, recorded in Section~\ref{sec:setup} and
Appendix~\ref{sec:app_details}. Beyond those traces we did not use generative AI to generate data, to formulate or prove
mathematical claims, or to translate. The authors verified every reported number and take full responsibility for the paper.
