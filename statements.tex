\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
Every number in the paper is produced by a script from a frozen artifact. The public repository
contains the measurement suite, the recorded development and confirmation results with their
registration records, the generators that write each table and figure, and the tests that check
the printed values against their sources. Section~\ref{sec:setup} and
Appendix~\ref{sec:app_details} give the probe sets, the compression protocols, the checkpoints and
the evaluation rules, and Appendix~\ref{sec:app_registration} records the registration order.
Complete result tables and auxiliary analyses are provided as supplementary material.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
The study uses publicly released models and public benchmark data, and involves no human subjects
and no personal data. Compressed models can lose capabilities unevenly; the
capability-conditioned endpoint of this paper makes such losses measurable before deployment, and
we see no direct negative application of the results.
