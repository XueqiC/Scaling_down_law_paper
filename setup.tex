\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\subsection{Capability-Conditioned Loss}
\label{sec:loss}

We measure three capabilities, mathematical reasoning, code
generation, and multi-hop question answering, each through a fixed
probe set $\probeset{c}$ of prompt and reference pairs drawn once
from held-out splits of MATH-500
\citep{hendrycks2021math,lightman2023verify}, MBPP
\citep{austin2021program}, and 2WikiMultihopQA
\citep{ho2020constructing}. Each probe set fixes one evaluation distribution; we write $L_c$ with it implied. The observable
is the
token-weighted conditional cross-entropy of the reference completion for a model with parameters $w$,
\begin{equation}
\Lc{c}(w)\;=\;\frac{1}{T_c}\sum_{(x,y)\in\probeset{c}}\;\sum_{t=1}^{|y|}
-\log p_w\!\left(y_t\mid x,y_{<t}\right),\qquad
T_c=\sum_{(x,y)\in\probeset{c}}|y|,
\label{eq:taskloss}
\end{equation}
with prompt tokens masked and the same 64 probes per capability for each state compared within a
model. The reference model $M_0$ is the checkpoint before the compression intervention. Responses are within-model differences in native nats per token: the damage
$\Delta L_c=L_c(\text{compressed})-L_c(M_0)$ for pruning and quantization, and for distillation the
signed transfer $\delta_c=L_c(S_{\mathrm{KD}})-L_c(S_0)$ of a student relative to its own initial
state, the reference of that arm; the teacher is not used as the source. The observable is a
likelihood; accuracies are not used, and absolute levels from different tokenizers are not placed
on one scale, so comparisons are always made within a model.

\subsection{Models and Compression Settings}
\label{sec:models}

A \emph{heterogeneous panel} of twelve public models (0.6B to 32B) establishes the phenomena and
the limits of cross-family transfer. A \emph{controlled panel} of
Pythia checkpoints \citep{biderman2023pythia} (160M, 410M, 1.4B at steps 16k, 64k, 143k) varies
size and stage independently and is the development set for pruning and quantization; distillation
is developed on Gemma-3 students. Held-out Pythia states (160M to 6.9B), new configurations and new
pools provide the validation of the frozen relations on states and settings that the fits never
saw.

The three operations are fixed by protocol: global magnitude pruning to a retained density $d$;
symmetric round-to-nearest quantization per output channel at bit-width $b$ or with groups of $g$
weights sharing one scale; and LoRA fine-tuning \citep{hu2022lora} of an existing student on
black-box teacher traces from a pool of $U$ traces per domain for a fixed budget
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app_details}). The inputs admitted to each predictor are the parameter count $N_0$
of the transformer weight matrices of $M_0$ (total and per-token
active counts coincide for the dense models studied here), the
pretraining token count $D_0$ where disclosed, and the reference
capability losses $L_{0,c}=L_c(M_0)$ measured once (the initial
student for distillation). Each prediction is written as
\begin{equation}
\widehat L_{m,c}\;=\;L^{\mathrm{obs}}_{0,c}\;+\;
\widehat\Delta_{m,c}(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\theta_m),\qquad
\mathbf x=(N_0,D_0,L_{0,c}),
\label{eq:interface}
\end{equation}
with $\boldsymbol\theta_m$ the setting of method $m$ (density,
bit-width and group size, or pool and budget).

\subsection{Fitting and Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

Each relation is fit on development states and settings only, with leave-one-stage-out and
leave-one-source-out folds, and judged on unseen settings and states with forms, coefficients and
predictions committed before the outcome is measured; each prediction keeps its status
(development, frozen prediction, post-hoc recommendation) regardless of the outcome. Error is the mean absolute error of $\widehat\Delta$ in nats per token per capability, compared
on the same cells with source-free references, same-input alternatives and the no-$D_0$ ablation;
the strongest baseline chosen inside the development folds is the comparator, and any baseline
chosen after the fact is marked as such. Freeze commits, provenance codes, fold rules and
bootstrap units are in Appendix~\ref{sec:app_registration}.
