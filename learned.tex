\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:learned}

This study developed predictive scaling-down laws for capability-conditioned loss under pruning,
quantization and distillation under one validation standard. Three rules organise the results: a
law may share a response shape only across capabilities whose shapes agree; interpolating within a
measured model and transferring to a new model state are different problems with different
admissible inputs; and the data requirement of distillation is a joint property of training
exposure, student and evaluation distribution. On these rules rest the relations of
Table~\ref{tab:main-prediction-v2}, the displacement account of damage, and a selection rule
confirmed prospectively on fresh states.
