\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:learned}

This study developed predictive scaling-down laws for capability-conditioned loss under pruning,
quantization and distillation, organised by three rules. A law may share a response shape only
across capabilities whose shapes agree. Interpolation within a measured model and transfer to a
new state admit different inputs. The data requirement of distillation depends jointly on training
exposure, student and evaluation distribution. On these rules rest the relations of
Table~\ref{tab:main-prediction-v2} and a selection rule confirmed prospectively in two families.
