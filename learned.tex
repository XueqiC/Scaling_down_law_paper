\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:learned}

Making the capability cost of compression predictable returns three things. The investigation
shows which response shapes capabilities share: mathematics and code admit one shape under pruning
with a capability-specific scale, question answering needs its own, and the cost of reusing
distillation data depends on the student and the evaluation distribution. The relations turn those
observations into forms that can be fitted and tested, and inside the controlled pruning domain a
compact relation matches a regression fitted on twice the development measurements. The selection
rule carries them into a decision and recovers most of the cross-method benefit available for
question answering under a storage constraint. Three boundaries hold: measurement efficiency is
not stable transfer, configuration range, student state and evaluation distribution bound each
relation, and the selection evidence holds for the candidate sets and budgets tested.
