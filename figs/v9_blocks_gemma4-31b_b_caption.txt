Capability regions in gradient space for Gemma4-31B: pairwise similarity of per-benchmark gradient signatures under four metrics (raw Fisher cosine, log-Fisher cosine, shared-component-removed log-Fisher cosine, top-0.1% coordinate Jaccard). Axis-label hue denotes capability; matrix intensity denotes similarity. Each metric uses one unchanged shared scale across all ten models.
