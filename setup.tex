\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

This section fixes the quantity we predict, the models and settings on which we measure it, and the
rule by which a prediction counts as validated.

\subsection{Capability-Conditioned Loss}
\label{sec:loss}

We measure mathematical reasoning (Math), code generation (Code) and multi-hop question answering
(QA) through fixed probe sets $\probeset{c}$ drawn once from held-out splits of MATH-500
\citep{hendrycks2021math,lightman2023verify}, MBPP \citep{austin2021program} and 2WikiMultihopQA
\citep{ho2020constructing}; each fixes one evaluation distribution, implied in $L_c$. The observable
is the
token-weighted conditional cross-entropy of the reference completion for a model with parameters $w$,
\begin{equation}
\Lc{c}(w)\;=\;\frac{1}{T_c}\sum_{(x,y)\in\probeset{c}}\;\sum_{t=1}^{|y|}
-\log p_w\!\left(y_t\mid x,y_{<t}\right),\qquad
T_c=\sum_{(x,y)\in\probeset{c}}|y|,
\label{eq:taskloss}
\end{equation}
with prompt tokens masked and the same 64 probes per capability within a model. The reference model
$M_0$ is the checkpoint before the intervention, and responses are within-model differences in
native nats per token: the damage $\Delta L_c=L_c(\text{compressed})-L_c(M_0)$ for pruning and
quantization, and for distillation the signed transfer
$\delta_c=L_c(S_{\mathrm{KD}})-L_c(S_0)$ of a student against its own initial state. Absolute levels from different tokenizers are never placed on one scale.

\subsection{Models and Compression Settings}
\label{sec:models}

A \emph{heterogeneous panel} of twelve public models from 0.6B to 32B establishes the phenomena.
A \emph{controlled panel} of Pythia checkpoints \citep{biderman2023pythia}, three sizes at three
training stages, varies size and stage independently and is the development set for pruning and
quantization; distillation is developed on Gemma-3 students. Held-out Pythia states up to 6.9B, new
configurations and new pools provide the validation of the frozen relations.

Three operations are fixed by protocol (Appendix~\ref{sec:app_details}): global magnitude pruning
to a retained density $d$; symmetric round-to-nearest quantization per output channel at bit-width
$b$, or with groups of $g$ weights sharing one scale; and LoRA fine-tuning \citep{hu2022lora} of an
existing student on teacher traces. The inputs admitted to each predictor are the parameter count $N_0$ of the transformer weight
matrices of $M_0$, the pretraining token count $D_0$ where disclosed, and the reference capability
losses $L_{0,c}=L_c(M_0)$ measured once; for distillation the initial student plays this role. Each prediction is written as
\begin{equation}
\widehat L_{m,c}\;=\;L^{\mathrm{obs}}_{0,c}\;+\;
\widehat\Delta_{m,c}(\mathbf x,\boldsymbol\theta_m),\qquad
\mathbf x=(N_0,D_0,L_{0,c}),
\label{eq:interface}
\end{equation}
with $\boldsymbol\theta_m$ the setting of method $m$: density, bit-width and group size, or pool
and budget.

\subsection{Fitting and Evaluation}
\label{sec:eval}

Each relation is fit on development states and settings only, with leave-one-stage-out and
leave-one-source-out folds, and judged on unseen settings and states with forms, coefficients and
predictions committed beforehand; each prediction keeps the status it was given, whatever the
outcome. Error is the mean absolute error of $\widehat\Delta$ in nats per token per capability on the same
cells as source-free references, same-input alternatives and the no-$D_0$ ablation; the strongest baseline chosen inside the development folds is the comparator.
Appendix~\ref{sec:app_registration} lists freeze commits and fold rules.
