\section{What Was Learned, and What It Enables}
\label{sec:learned}

\paragraph{Responses and relations.} Compression damages capabilities unevenly and the unevenness is
structured: a capability-specific power form under pruning inside its density range, a
configuration-dependent threshold under quantization that the measured grid predicts better than any
surface, and a distillation reuse cost that grows with reuse and student size and transfers across
QA distributions while the gain does not (Table~\ref{tab:main-prediction-v2}).

\paragraph{Understanding gained in trying to build reliable laws.} First, a rejected form is not a rejected class: the logarithmic reuse term failed its own flatness
implication, yet the fit-free second-difference test on two students did not reject additivity of
budget and reuse on the QA readout (\S\ref{sec:why}). Second, an explanation is not a
predictor: the second-order displacement account reproduces damage across three families inside a
magnitude boundary and needs the compressed model to compute. Third, source information transfers less than it fits: dense
pre-compression statistics improve a parametric family in four of nine pairs and lose to a
source-free configuration curve in nine of nine, with or without a shrunk source correction. Fourth, identifiability is a
property of the design: the data that fitted a curvature exponent near one contained no
budget-by-reuse rectangle, so the one fit-free statistic that separates additive from interacting
structures was uncomputable until four trajectories were designed to land on the corners.

\paragraph{Guidance for design and choice.} Choose inputs by the transfer needed: the initial state
carries math and code at unseen densities and group sizes of seen states and over-predicts QA under
pruning on new checkpoints, so a calibrated configuration median is the safer default when the state
is new. Fix the training protocol before measuring distillation damage; the learning rate moves it by
an order of magnitude. Design data-reuse experiments so that a budget-by-reuse rectangle exists by
construction. Pay for calibration by what it buys: one point on the target distribution closes most
of the gap to a source-specific fit, and dense statistics that cost more did not earn it. State the
range with the relation, as Table~\ref{tab:main-prediction-v2} does.

\paragraph{Limits.} The relations are conditional on the evaluated distributions and
implementations. No fitted joint structure for distillation beat a low-order response surface on
either pre-declared target, so the data requirement a practitioner would most like is supportable in
form and not yet delivered as a validated relation; the larger student diverged from the smaller on
math and code at readouts the design could not resolve. Reopening measurement needs a new
information source or a mechanism hypothesis, not another candidate reuse function.
