\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Existing compression laws take sparsity \citep{frantar2023scaling}, precision
\citep{kumar2024scaling}, training formats \citep{panferov2025unified}, the pre-pruning loss and
post-training tokens \citep{zhang2024scaling}, or the teacher loss \citep{busbridge2025distillation}
as inputs and predict an aggregate loss; we follow the P$^2$ law in admitting the pre-compression
loss and add a per-capability endpoint, a panel in which size and stage vary separately, and
held-out tests against same-input baselines. The fixed operations stand in for method families in
pruning \citep{lecun1989optimal,han2015deep,frankle2019lottery,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple,ma2023llmpruner,ashkboos2024slicegpt,men2024shortgpt},
post-training quantization
\citep{dettmers2022llmint8,frantar2022gptq,xiao2022smoothquant,lin2023awq,chee2023quip,shao2023omniquant,dettmers2023qlora},
and distillation
\citep{hinton2015distilling,sanh2019distilbert,hsieh2023distilling,mukherjee2023orca,gu2023minillm,agarwal2023gkd,xu2024survey}.
Uneven damage is documented for single tasks and quantizers
\citep{jaiswal2023compressing,liu2025quantization,ouyang2024lowbit,hong2024decoding,sengupta2025compression};
this study adds one endpoint and one validation standard across the three methods. Pretraining loss follows power laws
\citep{hestness2017deep,kaplan2020scaling,hoffmann2022training}, downstream behaviour is predictable
mainly through log-likelihood \citep{schaeffer2023emergent,gadre2024language,bhagia2024establishing,lourie2025scaling},
fitted forms depend on the fitting protocol \citep{li2025misfitting,besiroglu2024chinchilla}, and
repeated tokens are worth less than new ones \citep{muennighoff2023scaling}, which
\S\ref{sec:observed} quantifies per capability, student and evaluation distribution.
