\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Existing compression laws take sparsity, precision, training formats, the pre-pruning loss and
post-training tokens, or the teacher loss as inputs and predict an aggregate loss
\citep{frantar2023scaling,kumar2024scaling,panferov2025unified,zhang2024scaling,busbridge2025distillation};
this study adds a per-capability endpoint, a panel in which size and stage vary separately, and
held-out tests against same-input baselines. The fixed operations stand in for method families in pruning, post-training quantization and
distillation
\citep{lecun1989optimal,frankle2019lottery,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple,frantar2022gptq,xiao2022smoothquant,lin2023awq,dettmers2023qlora,hinton2015distilling,hsieh2023distilling,gu2023minillm,agarwal2023gkd},
and uneven damage is documented for single tasks and quantizers
\citep{jaiswal2023compressing,liu2025quantization,sengupta2025compression}. Pretraining loss follows power laws and downstream behaviour is predictable mainly through
log-likelihood
\citep{kaplan2020scaling,hoffmann2022training,schaeffer2023emergent,bhagia2024establishing},
with fitted forms depending on the protocol \citep{li2025misfitting,besiroglu2024chinchilla}.
Repeated tokens are worth less than new ones \citep{muennighoff2023scaling}, a cost
\S\ref{sec:observed} resolves per capability and student.
