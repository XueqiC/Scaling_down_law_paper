\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Scaling Laws for LLM Compression.}
Compression laws take sparsity, precision, training format, the pre-pruning loss and post-training
tokens, or the teacher loss as inputs and predict an aggregate loss
\citep{frantar2023scaling,kumar2024scaling,panferov2025unified,zhang2024scaling,busbridge2025distillation},
following pretraining laws in which loss follows power laws in scale and downstream behaviour tracks
log-likelihood
\citep{hestness2017deep,kaplan2020scaling,hoffmann2022training,schaeffer2023emergent,gadre2024language,bhagia2024establishing,lourie2025scaling},
with a fitted form that depends on the protocol \citep{li2025misfitting,besiroglu2024chinchilla}.
Closest to ours, \citet{sengupta2025compression} predict a task's post-pruning accuracy from its unpruned accuracy and the pruning ratio, transferring the exponent to unseen models with or without one calibrating measurement, and \citet{zhou2026task} fit task-stratified quantization laws that join model size, bit width, group size and calibration-set size, validated at unseen sizes. Capability-specific responses and the joint action of bit width and group size are therefore established; this paper adds how much information and how many measurements a capability-loss prediction needs, separated into unseen configurations and model-state transfer across three methods, and the prediction's decision value.

\paragraph{Compression Effects on Model Capabilities.}
The fixed operations we study stand in for the method families of pruning, post-training
quantization and distillation
\citep{lecun1989optimal,han2015deep,frankle2019lottery,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple,ma2023llmpruner,ashkboos2024slicegpt,men2024shortgpt,dettmers2022llmint8,frantar2022gptq,xiao2022smoothquant,lin2023awq,chee2023quip,shao2023omniquant,dettmers2023qlora,hinton2015distilling,sanh2019distilbert,hsieh2023distilling,mukherjee2023orca,gu2023minillm,agarwal2023gkd,xu2024survey},
and uneven capability loss is documented for individual tasks and quantizers
\citep{jaiswal2023compressing,liu2025quantization,ouyang2024lowbit,hong2024decoding,sengupta2025compression}.

\paragraph{Compression Method Selection.}
Prior work compares compression methods by measuring each candidate on the target model, finding
quantization to retain more than pruning at the same ratio \citep{jaiswal2023compressing} or placing
sparsity and precision on one capacity scale \citep{panferov2025unified}; repeated tokens are worth
less than new ones \citep{muennighoff2023scaling}, an exchange rate our reuse response measures per
capability and student (Section~\ref{sec:observed}). None states which method a given model,
capability and budget should receive without measuring every candidate; Section~\ref{sec:selection}
tests that decision on independent candidate sets.
