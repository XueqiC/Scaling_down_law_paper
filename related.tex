\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Existing compression laws take sparsity \citep{frantar2023scaling}, precision
\citep{kumar2024scaling}, training formats \citep{panferov2025unified}, the pre-pruning loss and
post-training tokens \citep{zhang2024scaling}, or the teacher loss
\citep{busbridge2025distillation} as inputs and predict an aggregate loss; we follow the P$^2$ law in
admitting the pre-compression loss and add a per-capability endpoint, a panel in which size and stage
vary separately, and held-out tests against same-input baselines. The fixed operations stand in for
method families in pruning
\citep{lecun1989optimal,han2015deep,frankle2019lottery,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple,ma2023llmpruner,ashkboos2024slicegpt,men2024shortgpt},
post-training quantization
\citep{dettmers2022llmint8,frantar2022gptq,xiao2022smoothquant,lin2023awq,chee2023quip,shao2023omniquant,dettmers2023qlora},
and distillation
\citep{hinton2015distilling,sanh2019distilbert,hsieh2023distilling,mukherjee2023orca,gu2023minillm,agarwal2023gkd,xu2024survey}.
That compression harms capabilities unevenly is documented for knowledge-intensive tasks under
pruning \citep{jaiswal2023compressing}, reasoning under low-bit quantization
\citep{liu2025quantization,ouyang2024lowbit}, and trust dimensions \citep{hong2024decoding}, and
task-level laws exist for single quantizers and per-task pruning exponents
\citep{sengupta2025compression}; what is missing, and what this study supplies, is one endpoint and
one validation standard across the three methods with the transfer range of each relation
measured. On the fitting side, pretraining loss follows power laws
\citep{hestness2017deep,kaplan2020scaling,hoffmann2022training}, downstream behaviour is predictable
mainly through log-likelihood
\citep{schaeffer2023emergent,gadre2024language,bhagia2024establishing,lourie2025scaling}, fitted
forms are fragile to the fitting protocol \citep{li2025misfitting,besiroglu2024chinchilla}, and
repeated tokens are worth less than new ones \citep{muennighoff2023scaling}, which the reuse
results of \S\ref{sec:observed} quantify per capability and student. Gradient importance
\citep{lecun1989optimal,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple} enters only as mechanism evidence
(Appendix~\ref{sec:app_mech}).
