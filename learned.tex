\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:learned}

This study developed predictive scaling-down laws for capability-conditioned loss under pruning,
quantization and distillation, organised by three rules: a law may share a response shape only
across capabilities whose shapes agree; interpolating within a measured model and transferring to a
new state are different problems with different admissible inputs; and the data requirement of
distillation depends jointly on training exposure, student and evaluation distribution. On them
rest the relations of Table~\ref{tab:main-prediction-v2}, the displacement account of damage and a selection rule confirmed prospectively in two model families on candidates frozen in advance.
