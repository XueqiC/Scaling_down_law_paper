\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
Every number in the paper is produced by a script from a fixed result file; the public repository contains the measurement suite, the recorded development and confirmation results with the record of what was fixed and when, the generators of every table and figure, and the tests that check printed values against their sources. Section~\ref{sec:setup} and
Appendix~\ref{sec:app_details} give the probe sets, the compression protocols, the checkpoints and
the evaluation rules, and Appendix~\ref{sec:app_registration} records the order in which the predictions were fixed.
Complete result tables and auxiliary analyses are provided as supplementary material.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
The study uses publicly released models and public benchmark data, and involves no human subjects
and no personal data. Compressed models can lose capabilities unevenly; measuring that before deployment is the purpose of this paper, and we see no direct negative application of the results.
