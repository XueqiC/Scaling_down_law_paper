\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
Every number in the paper is produced by a script from a frozen artifact. The public repository
contains the measurement suite, the development and confirmation results with their registration
records and hashes, the generators that write each table and figure from those artifacts with a
sidecar naming the source of each printed value, and the tests that check the printed values
against their sources. Section~\ref{sec:setup} and Appendix~\ref{sec:app_details} give the probe
sets, the compression protocols, the checkpoints and the fitting and evaluation rules;
Appendix~\ref{sec:app_registration} records the registration order and the data cut-off. Complete
result tables and auxiliary analyses are provided as supplementary material.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
The study uses publicly released models and public benchmark data, involves no human subjects and
no personal data, and reports measurements of model capabilities under compression. Compressed
models can lose capabilities unevenly; the capability-conditioned endpoint of this paper makes such
losses measurable before deployment, and we see no direct negative application of the results.

\section*{AI Use Statement}
In this work, we used generative AI tools for implementing methods (the measurement, analysis and
figure code, verified by unit tests and by regenerating every reported number from the frozen
artifacts), for designing and providing feedback on experiments (including the corner design of
\S\ref{sec:why}, which the authors reviewed and registered before the trajectories were run), for
interpreting results (draft readings that the authors checked against the pre-registered
criteria), and for drafting and editing the text. We have not used generative AI tools to generate
synthetic data sets, to formulate or prove mathematical claims, or to translate; qualitative and
thematic data analysis is not applicable to this work. The authors verified every reported number
against its source artifact and take full responsibility for the content of the paper.
