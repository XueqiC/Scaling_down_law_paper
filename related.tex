\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Scaling Laws for LLM Compression.}
Compression laws take sparsity, precision, training format, the pre-pruning loss and post-training
tokens, or the teacher loss as inputs and predict an aggregate loss
\citep{frantar2023scaling,kumar2024scaling,panferov2025unified,zhang2024scaling,busbridge2025distillation},
in the tradition of pretraining laws, where loss follows power laws in scale and downstream behaviour is
predictable mainly through log-likelihood
\citep{hestness2017deep,kaplan2020scaling,hoffmann2022training,schaeffer2023emergent,gadre2024language,bhagia2024establishing,lourie2025scaling},
and the fitted form depends on the fitting protocol \citep{li2025misfitting,besiroglu2024chinchilla}.
We keep that goal and change the endpoint to a per-capability one, on a panel in which model size and
training stage vary separately.

\paragraph{Compression Effects on Model Capabilities.}
The fixed operations we study stand in for the method families of pruning, post-training
quantization and distillation
\citep{lecun1989optimal,han2015deep,frankle2019lottery,frantar2023sparsegpt,sun2023simple,ma2023llmpruner,ashkboos2024slicegpt,men2024shortgpt,dettmers2022llmint8,frantar2022gptq,xiao2022smoothquant,lin2023awq,chee2023quip,shao2023omniquant,dettmers2023qlora,hinton2015distilling,sanh2019distilbert,hsieh2023distilling,mukherjee2023orca,gu2023minillm,agarwal2023gkd,xu2024survey},
and uneven capability loss is documented for individual tasks and quantizers
\citep{jaiswal2023compressing,liu2025quantization,ouyang2024lowbit,hong2024decoding,sengupta2025compression}.
Those measurements establish that the effect is uneven; Section~\ref{sec:observed} measures its shape
across methods, model states and evaluation distributions on one endpoint.

\paragraph{Compression Method Selection.}
Repeated tokens are worth less than new ones \citep{muennighoff2023scaling}, which
Section~\ref{sec:observed} resolves per capability and student. Choosing between methods needs predictions
comparable across them, which Section~\ref{sec:selection} supplies and evaluates against a single-method policy under a storage
constraint on four independent references.
