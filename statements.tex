\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
Every number is produced by a script from a fixed result file; the public repository holds the
measurement suite, the recorded results, the table and figure generators, and tests of every printed
value. Section~\ref{sec:setup} and Appendices~\ref{sec:app_details} and~\ref{sec:app_registration} give
the protocols and when each prediction was fixed.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
The study uses public models and benchmark data, involves no human subjects or personal data, and has
no direct negative use that we foresee.
