\section{What Was Learned, and What It Enables}
\label{sec:learned}

Three kinds of result come out of this study, and each rests on evidence a reader can locate.

\paragraph{Responses and relations.} Compression damages capabilities unevenly, and the unevenness is
itself structured. Under global magnitude pruning the response of math and code to density follows a
five-parameter power form with a source-conditioned amplitude inside $d\in[0.6,0.9]$, matching a
per-density regression on three new checkpoints (0.24 against 0.23 nats for math) and losing its
footing outside that range and on QA (\S\ref{sec:laws}). Under grouped quantization the configuration
axis carries the prediction: a frozen same-input interpolation over the measured grid predicts unseen
group sizes on the development states where a parametric surface does not, and per-channel
quantization supports only a per-bit source regression. Under a fixed trace-distillation protocol the
response to independent-data reuse is large, monotone and size-scaling: at a matched budget of about
200k supervised tokens, reusing a small pool costs QA $+0.29$, $+2.55$ and $+4.85$ nats on students of
100M, 700M and 3.2B non-embedding parameters, while nine times more independent data turns the same
budget into a gain of about one nat on each (\S\ref{sec:observed}). The cost of over-reuse reproduces
on three QA distributions; the gain is confined to 2Wiki.

\paragraph{Understanding gained in trying to build reliable laws.} Four lessons carry beyond this
paper. First, a rejected form is not a rejected class: the four-coefficient joint form with a
logarithmic reuse term failed its own flatness implication, yet the fit-free second-difference test on
two students did not reject additivity of budget and reuse on the QA readout, with the measured
statistic between the additive prediction of zero and the frozen interaction candidate's $-0.49$ nats,
about one and a half noise units from each (\S\ref{sec:why}). Second, an explanation is not a
predictor: the second-order logit-displacement account reproduces compression damage across three
families with one boundary in damage magnitude, and it needs the compressed model to compute, so it
explains without forecasting. Third, source information transfers less than it fits: dense
pre-compression statistics improve a parametric family in four of nine arm-and-capability pairs and
lose to a source-free median configuration curve in nine of nine, and holding that curve fixed while
adding only a shrunk source correction gains nothing on the primary split. Fourth, identifiability is a
property of the design, not the estimator: a curvature exponent profiled inside folds sits near one
rather than zero, but its fold range is wide, and the data set that produced it contained no complete
budget-by-reuse rectangle, so the one statistic that discriminates additive from interacting
structures without fitting was uncomputable until four trajectories were designed to land on the
corners.

\paragraph{Guidance for design and choice.} The practical content is concrete. Choose inputs by the
transfer you need: the initial state carries math and code at unseen densities and group sizes of seen
states, adds nothing on new quantization states, and over-predicts QA under pruning on every new
checkpoint; a configuration median with a one-point calibration is the safer default when the state is
new. Fix the training protocol before measuring distillation damage, because the learning rate
changes the damage magnitude by an order of magnitude on the 1B student while leaving the size
ordering intact. Design data-reuse experiments so that at least one budget-by-reuse rectangle exists by
construction, since fixed pools and a doubling budget grid never produce one. Budget calibration
honestly: the one-point calibration that closes most of the gap to a source-specific fit costs one
forward pass on the target distribution, and the dense statistics that cost more did not earn it.
State the range with the relation: every relation here carries the states, configurations,
capabilities and distributions it was tested on, and the decision study covers four Pythia states
under nominal storage budgets with its cross-family validation on fresh candidates not completed.

\paragraph{Limits, and what would reopen the experiments.} The relations are conditional on the
evaluated loss distributions and the compression implementations. No fitted joint structure for
distillation beat a low-order response surface on either pre-declared target, so the data requirement
a practitioner would most like, how much independent data keeps a conditional loss below a threshold,
is supportable in form and not yet delivered as a validated relation. The larger student diverged from
the smaller on math and code at readouts the design could not resolve. Reopening measurement would
require a new information source or a mechanism hypothesis rather than another candidate reuse
function; the remaining budget is not by itself a reason.
