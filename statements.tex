\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
Every number in the paper is produced by a script from a fixed result file; the public repository holds
the measurement suite, the recorded results, the generators of every table and figure, and tests that
check printed values against their sources. Section~\ref{sec:setup} and Appendix~\ref{sec:app_details}
give the protocols, and Appendix~\ref{sec:app_registration} the order in which predictions were fixed.

\section*{Ethics Statement}
The study uses public models and benchmark data, involves no human subjects or personal data, and has
no direct negative use that we foresee; measuring uneven capability loss before deployment is its aim.
