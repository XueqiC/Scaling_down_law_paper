\section*{AI Use Statement}
In this work, we used generative AI tools to polish the writing and to help implement experiment
code, which we verified by unit tests and by regenerating every reported number from the frozen
artifacts. The research questions, the experimental design, the analysis and the scientific
writing are the authors' own. The distillation experiments are a separate matter: their training
data are teacher traces generated by a commercial language model through its interface, which is
the object of study rather than an authoring aid, and \S\ref{sec:setup} and
Appendix~\ref{sec:app_details} record the model, the prompts and the pools. Beyond those traces we
have not used generative AI tools to generate data, to formulate or prove mathematical claims, or
to translate. The authors verified every reported number against its source artifact and take full
responsibility for the content of the paper.
